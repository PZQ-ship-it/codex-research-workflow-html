# Program Research Schema

Use this structure for researching a specific academic degree program. Verify official information first whenever web access is available.

## 1. Basic Program Information

- Degree type
- University
- Department, faculty, school, or divinity/law/arts unit
- Official program page
- Admissions page
- Application deadline

## 2. Academic Structure

- Program length
- Coursework requirements
- Exam requirements
- Thesis, dissertation, studio, portfolio, capstone, or research requirements
- Practicum, field education, clinical, language, teaching, or fieldwork requirements where relevant
- Residency or full-time enrollment requirements

## 3. Faculty And Departmental Resources

- Relevant faculty members
- Faculty research areas
- Recent publications where useful
- Research centers, labs, institutes, workshops, seminars, lecture series, conferences, archives, collections, or community partnerships
- Cross-registration or consortium options where relevant

## 4. Reputation And Rankings

- University ranking when useful
- Department or field-specific ranking where relevant
- Disciplinary reputation
- Treat rankings as secondary to faculty fit, funding, placement, and program structure

## 5. Online Reputation And Student Experience

- GradCafe, Reddit, The Student Room, forums, blogs, and social media may be used as anecdotal sources only
- Separate verified facts from anecdotal impressions
- Note sample-size and selection-bias risks

## 6. Cost And Funding

- Tuition
- Mandatory fees
- Estimated living costs
- Scholarships and fellowships
- Assistantships
- Tuition remission
- Stipend information
- External funding possibilities
- Financial risks for domestic and international students

## 7. Application Requirements

- Transcript
- GPA threshold if officially stated
- Language score requirements
- Writing sample requirements
- CV or resume
- Statement of purpose
- Personal statement
- Research proposal
- Recommendation letters
- Portfolio, audition, interview, GRE/GMAT/LSAT, or other materials where relevant
- Word limits and prompt requirements

## 8. Placement Record

- Official placement page or report if available
- Career outcomes
- PhD placement, academic jobs, industry outcomes, nonprofit/public sector outcomes, ministry/professional outcomes, or professional school outcomes where relevant
- If data is unavailable, say so clearly

## Output Standard

Include source links where available. Mark each claim as official, secondary, anecdotal, or unverified when the distinction matters.
