# Research Proposal Guide

Use this when the application requires a proposed research project or when the SOP needs a stronger project paragraph.

## Core Components

- Working title or topic
- Research question
- Field, corpus, archive, dataset, lab problem, case, site, or material
- Literature gap or unresolved problem
- Methodology
- Feasibility
- Contribution
- Fit with faculty and program resources

## Degree-Level Calibration

- MA/MPhil/STM/ThM: show a feasible project and preparation without pretending to have a finished dissertation.
- PhD: show a sustained research agenda, relevant preparation, and faculty fit.
- MFA: show artistic questions, practice, influences, portfolio fit, and future development.
- Professional degrees: show how research or inquiry supports practice.

## Method Expectations

- Humanities: sources, texts, archives, languages, theories, historical scope.
- Social sciences: research design, data, cases, methods, ethics, feasibility.
- STEM: hypothesis, technical approach, lab fit, prior work, methods, expected challenges.
- Theology/religious studies: languages, traditions, texts, methods, field placement or ministry context where relevant.

## Common Weaknesses

- Topic is too broad.
- Research question is a theme, not a question.
- Method is missing.
- Faculty fit is name-dropping.
- Contribution is inflated.
- Feasibility is ignored.
