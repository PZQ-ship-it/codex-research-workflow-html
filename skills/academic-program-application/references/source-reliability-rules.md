# Source Reliability Rules

Use source hierarchy explicitly when giving program-specific advice.

## Highest Reliability

- Official program pages
- Department and faculty pages
- Graduate admissions pages
- Registrar tuition pages
- Financial aid and funding pages
- Official placement pages
- Official course catalogues

Use these for requirements, deadlines, tuition, funding, faculty status, degree structure, and placement.

## Medium Reliability

- Professional association pages
- Field-specific rankings and directories
- Published department newsletters
- Faculty CVs, lab pages, and personal academic websites
- Peer-reviewed publications and academic profiles

Use these for context, faculty fit, disciplinary reputation, and research resources.

## Secondary Context Only

- University-wide rankings
- Department rankings
- News articles
- Blogs by current or former students

Use only to contextualize, never to override official requirements or applicant fit.

## Anecdotal Only

- GradCafe
- Reddit
- The Student Room
- Discord/Slack/forum screenshots
- Social media posts

Use only for possible student-experience impressions. Never treat them as authoritative. Do not infer admissions odds from small samples.

## Required Uncertainty Labels

Use these labels when needed:

- Verified official: found on official university or department source
- Secondary context: found on ranking, professional, or news source
- Anecdotal: found in forum, blog, or social media source
- Unverified: user-provided or web-reported claim not confirmed
- Not found: searched but did not locate the information
- Unclear: official information exists but is ambiguous or conflicting

## Prohibited Behavior

- Do not invent deadlines, faculty interests, funding guarantees, stipend amounts, placement outcomes, admissions statistics, or requirements.
- Do not treat "fully funded" as true unless the official source states the funding structure.
- Do not assume a faculty member is accepting students unless confirmed or clearly framed as a question to verify.
