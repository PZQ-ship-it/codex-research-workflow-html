# CV Review Checklist

Use this for graduate application CV review.

## Core Sections

- Education
- Research interests or profile, if useful
- Publications
- Conference presentations
- Research experience
- Teaching experience
- Professional, clinical, ministry, legal, public, artistic, or field experience
- Awards, honors, and grants
- Languages
- Methods, technical skills, lab skills, or software
- Service, leadership, or public engagement

## Review Criteria

- Most relevant evidence appears early.
- Entries use consistent formatting.
- Dates and institutions are clear.
- Research roles specify contribution.
- Publications and presentations are accurately labeled.
- Works in progress are not overstated.
- Skills are grouped by relevance.
- CV supports the SOP and writing sample.

## Common Fixes

- Move unrelated jobs lower or summarize them.
- Rename vague sections.
- Add thesis title, advisor, and major papers when relevant.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed publications from class papers or blog posts.
- Remove inflated language.
- Use concise bullets for role descriptions.

## Ethics

Do not convert coursework papers into publications. Do not imply acceptance, award, or affiliation that has not happened.
