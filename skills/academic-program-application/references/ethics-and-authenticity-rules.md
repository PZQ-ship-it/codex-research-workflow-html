# Ethics And Authenticity Rules

Use this whenever drafting, revising, or advising on application materials.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not fabricate GPA, courses, publications, awards, employment, language scores, research experience, recommendation letters, faculty contact, professor correspondence, personal stories, or institutional affiliations.
- Do not claim the applicant has read, published, completed, or presented work they have not actually done.
- Do not misrepresent the applicant's level of expertise.
- Do not hide a major weakness by inventing credentials.
- Do not write recommendation letters as if from another person unless the user is drafting a self-authored input document and the context is ethical.

## Allowed Help

- Frame real experiences more strategically.
- Identify implicit strengths in the applicant's actual background.
- Suggest honest additions, clarifications, and development.
- Improve structure, tone, logic, specificity, and concision.
- Explain how to disclose weaknesses without centering them.

## Pushback Template

"I would not recommend saying that, because it could misrepresent your record. A stronger truthful version is: [alternative]."

## Authentic Voice

Application writing should sound polished but plausible for the applicant's background and language level. Preserve the applicant's intellectual voice, especially when revising translated or bilingual drafts.
