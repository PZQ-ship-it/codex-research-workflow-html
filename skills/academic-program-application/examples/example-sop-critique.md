# Example SOP Critique

## Overall Assessment

The draft has a coherent topic and credible preparation, but it needs sharper stakes and more specific faculty/program fit.

## Major Structural Problems

- The opening identifies a topic but not yet a sufficiently pointed research problem.
- The faculty paragraph is plausible but still slightly generic.
- The career goal is acceptable but could be more connected to the applicant's scholarly agenda.

## Argument / Narrative Problems

- The phrase "modern religious public" needs definition.
- The transition from MA thesis to PhD project should explain what changes in scale, sources, or method.
- The draft should clarify why Protestant periodicals matter beyond one denominational history.

## Discipline-Specific Problems

- For history, the draft needs clearer source base, periodization, and historiographical intervention.
- If applying to religious studies, the draft should also name conceptual debates in religion and modernity.

## Sentence-Level Issues

- Replace broad phrases like "closely relates" with concrete points of overlap.
- Avoid repeating "resources" in the program-fit paragraph.

## Revised Version

My proposed graduate study asks how Chinese Protestant periodicals in the Republican era helped produce new forms of religious public discourse. Rather than treating these periodicals only as denominational publications, I approach them as sites where Chinese editors, pastors, educators, and lay writers translated Christian concepts into debates about citizenship, social reform, education, and national belonging.

## Further Revision Suggestions

- Add two or three primary sources or archives.
- Name one historiographical debate.
- Make each faculty fit claim specific to method, period, or source base.
