Native Autoresearch Checklist

  • Mission, scope, and non-goals are explicit.
  • Validator mode is one of script-validator, rubric-review, or acceptance-artifact.
  • Completion artifact path is known.
  • Evidence ledger path is known.
  • Sources include quality/date/context notes.
  • Failed validation attempts are recorded.
  • Final status is passed or approved before completion is claimed.
