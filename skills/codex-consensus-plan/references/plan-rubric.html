Consensus Plan Rubric

  • The plan is grounded in inspected repo facts, not guesses.
  • The plan names defaults where user preferences were not required.
  • The implementer can execute without choosing architecture, scope, or tests.
  • RALPLAN-DR includes principles, top decision drivers, viable options, chosen direction, and rejected alternatives.
  • Architect review includes a strong counterargument, tradeoff tension, and synthesis when possible.
  • Critic review checks principle-option consistency, alternatives, risks, acceptance criteria, and verification.
  • ADR records decision, drivers, alternatives, why chosen, consequences, and follow-ups.
  • The test plan proves behavior, visual quality, or document consistency as appropriate.
  • High-risk plans include a pre-mortem and expanded test shape.
  • Out-of-scope runtime surfaces, migrations, or risky automation are explicit.
  • The next lane is recommended but not auto-executed.
