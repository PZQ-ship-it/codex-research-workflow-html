Visual Acceptance Checklist

  • Reference or acceptance target is named before judging.
  • Viewport/page matrix is explicit and includes desktop plus mobile when responsive.
  • Screenshots or rendered pages are fresh, saved, and inspectable.
  • No blank canvas, broken image, or missing asset.
  • No overlapping, clipped, or unreadable text.
  • Responsive pages have no accidental page-level horizontal scroll.
  • Important labels, legends, and units are visible.
  • Console has no relevant errors or warnings for browser pages.
  • Pixel diff, if used, supports rather than replaces visual judgment.
  • Remaining differences are documented and acceptable.
  • Screenshots are saved under the repo's established artifact directory.
