Best Practice Source Quality Checklist

  • Official docs, upstream source, standards, release notes, or maintainer guidance are checked first.
  • Source URLs are included for material claims.
  • Version and date context are stated.
  • Third-party summaries are labeled supplemental.
  • Repo-local facts are separated from external guidance.
  • Conflicts, stale evidence, or undocumented behavior are flagged.
  • The answer stops with a handoff instead of silently implementing.
