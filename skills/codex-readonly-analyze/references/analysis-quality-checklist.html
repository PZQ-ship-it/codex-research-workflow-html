Readonly Analysis Quality Checklist

  • The response answers the asked question before explaining process.
  • No files were edited.
  • Material claims cite concrete repo artifacts or commands.
  • Evidence, inference, and unknowns are separated.
  • Competing explanations are ranked by support.
  • Confidence reflects evidence strength, not rhetorical certainty.
  • Next steps are read-only probes unless the user asks to implement.
